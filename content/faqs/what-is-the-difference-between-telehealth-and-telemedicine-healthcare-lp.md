question: What is the difference between telehealth and telemedicine?
answer: While **telemedicine** focuses specifically on remote clinical services
  like virtual doctor consultations, **telehealth** is a broader term that
  encompasses **healthcare IT services, patient education, administrative
  functions, and remote monitoring**. A **telehealth app development company**
  creates solutions for both **telemedicine and telehealth**, ensuring seamless
  digital healthcare delivery.
