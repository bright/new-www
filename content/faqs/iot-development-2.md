question: What are IoT devices?
answer: IoT devices are smart objects equipped with sensors and internet
  connectivity that allow them to communicate with other devices or systems.
  These devices rely on **IoT cloud service providers** and **IoT integration
  services** for seamless operation. A smart home, for instance, uses various
  IoT devices like smart thermostats, lights, and security systems that work
  together through **IoT system integration services**.
