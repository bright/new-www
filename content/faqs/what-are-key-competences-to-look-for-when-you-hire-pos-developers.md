question: What are key competences to look for when you hire POS developers
answer: >-
  When you plan to **hire POS developers**, you should take into account a few
  crucial competences that go far beyond simple code writing.


  ### **Working in real environments under pressure**


  POS software is used daily by businesses, and any changes directly impact users. A skilled **POS system developer** must know how to work under pressure, quickly identify the source of issues, and fix bugs in production to minimize downtime. This is especially important with payments – sometimes problems come from payment providers, but it’s still the developer’s responsibility to trace the cause and coordinate with third parties to resolve the issue effectively.


  ### **Expertise in software and hardware integrations**


  Modern POS systems thrive on integrations. When you **hire POS developers**, make sure they are experienced in building custom integrations with a wide range of hardware (receipt printers, barcode scanners, card readers) as well as third-party services such as loyalty systems or food delivery platforms. Developers often need to contact third parties directly to clarify missing documentation and make informed business decisions to ensure stability and functionality.


  ### **Payment industry knowledge and compliance**


  A reliable **POS system developer** must understand modern security practices and compliance requirements such as PCI DSS compliance and EMV standards. When you **hire POS developers**, also check their experience with implementing contactless payment methods (like tap-to-pay).
