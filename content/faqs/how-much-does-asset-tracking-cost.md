question: How much does asset tracking cost?
answer: >-
  If you're considering building a tailored IoT asset tracking system, several
  factors can influence the cost and complexity of development. One key factor
  is the type of assets and data you want to monitor. For example, costs may be
  higher if you’re not only tracking equipment but also monitoring environmental
  conditions, such as temperature or [water quality
  monitoring](/projects/water-quality-monitoring/).


  The technology you choose also matters. Options include Bluetooth, GPS, Wi-Fi, or RFID – each with different use cases and price points. The level of customization plays a big role as well. Even the physical environment, such as a building’s construction or layout, can affect signal strength and connectivity.


  Because of these variables, estimating the cost of an asset tracking solution isn't straightforward. We recommend reaching out to our team, we’ll help assess your needs and provide a tailored estimate based on your specific use case.
