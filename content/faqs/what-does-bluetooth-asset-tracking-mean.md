question: What does Bluetooth asset tracking mean?
answer: Bluetooth asset tracking refers to using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
  technology to monitor the location and status of physical assets. BLE tags are
  attached to items, and nearby Bluetooth-enabled devices (like gateways or
  smartphones) detect and transmit data about those assets. Bluetooth asset
  tracking is ideal for real-time, indoor tracking of equipment, tools, or
  inventory with high accuracy and low power consumption.
