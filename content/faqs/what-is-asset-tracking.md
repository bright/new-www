question: What is asset tracking?
answer: Asset tracking is the process of monitoring and managing physical or
  digital assets using technologies like bluetooth, barcodes, RFID, or Wifi. It
  helps businesses gain visibility into asset location, usage, and condition –
  essential for efficient operations and inventory control.
