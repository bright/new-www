question: How to create an asset tracking system?
answer: >-
  To create an asset tracking system, you first need to define the types of
  assets you want to track. Then, determine whether you need more than just
  location data – perhaps your system should include IoT sensors to monitor
  temperature, humidity, or other environmental factors.


  Next, choose the right technology for your use case, such as Bluetooth, RFID, barcode, or Wi-Fi. From the very beginning, it's important to consider all specific aspects of your business: recurring challenges with asset management, the physical environment (like the layout of your building), and any tools or systems that need to be integrated with your custom asset tracking solution. All of these factors should be thoughtfully addressed before the first line of code is written.


  You'll also need to decide whether your internal team can handle the development, whether to outsource it to a specialized partner, or hire freelancers to support the project. Each option comes with its own timeline, cost, and risk profile, depending on your goals and technical capabilities.
