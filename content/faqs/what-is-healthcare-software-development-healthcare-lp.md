question: What is healthcare software development?
answer: Healthcare software development refers to the process of designing,
  building, and maintaining digital solutions for the healthcare industry. This
  includes **electronic health records (EHR) software, telemedicine platforms,
  and healthcare mobile application development**. A **healthcare software
  development company** specializes in creating tailored solutions to improve
  patient care, optimize workflows, and ensure regulatory compliance.
