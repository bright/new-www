question: What Is Point of Sale?
answer: >
  The **point of sale (POS)** refers to the moment and location where a
  transaction takes place between a customer and a business. A **POS system** is
  the technology that facilitates this process, enabling businesses to accept
  payments, track sales, manage inventory, and more. Modern **POS software
  development** allows companies to customize their systems for
  industry-specific needs, from retail and hospitality to food delivery and
  service-based businesses.
