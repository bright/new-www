question: What is a cloud-based POS system?
answer: >-
  A **cloud-based POS system** is a modern alternative to **traditional,
  on-premise POS software development**. Instead of storing data locally, these
  systems use **cloud technology** to manage **transactions, inventory, and
  reporting from anywhere**. Benefits include:


  * **remote access** to sales and business insights;

  * **automatic updates** and security enhancements;  

  * **scalability**, allowing businesses to expand effortlessly.
