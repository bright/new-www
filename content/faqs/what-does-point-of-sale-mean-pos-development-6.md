question: What Does Point of sale mean?
answer: When discussing **what is point of sale**, we’re referring to the entire
  system involved in processing customer purchases. It includes hardware like
  **card readers** and **POS terminals**, as well as software that manages
  transactions, customer data, and reporting. A well-designed **custom POS
  system** streamlines operations and enhances customer experience. **Point of
  sale customization** is usually provided by experienced **POS software
  developers** who leverage their expertise to integrate retail devices into
  **POS applications**, as well as design **secure payment systems** and more.
