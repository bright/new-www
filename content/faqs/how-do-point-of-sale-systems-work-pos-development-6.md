question: How do point of sale systems work?
answer: >
  Understanding **what is point of sale** also means knowing how **POS systems**
  function. A **POS system** consists of hardware and software working together
  to process sales. The steps typically include:


  1. The cashier scans or selects items for purchase.  

  2. The system calculates the total, including taxes and discounts.  

  3. The customer pays using a card, mobile payment, or cash.  

  4. The **POS terminal** processes the payment securely.  

  5. The system updates inventory and generates receipts.  


  With advancements in **POS application development**, businesses can integrate additional features like **analytics, automated invoicing, and loyalty programs**.
