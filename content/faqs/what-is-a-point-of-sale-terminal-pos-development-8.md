question: What is a point of sale terminal?
answer: >-
  A **POS terminal** is the hardware component of a **point of sale system**
  that facilitates payment processing. These devices can be **traditional card
  readers, touch-screen tablets, or all-in-one systems** that handle scanning,
  payments, and receipt printing. With **custom POS software development**,
  businesses can integrate additional features tailored to their specific needs.


  Usually, **POS developers** implement unique tweaks for a **custom point of sale** based on a POS vendor’s specifications and a deep understanding of the business domain.
