question: What is a healthcare asset tracking system?
answer: A **healthcare asset tracking system** is a technology-driven solution
  that helps medical institutions track and manage their physical assets, such
  as medical equipment, supplies, and devices. **Healthcare asset tracking**
  enhances operational efficiency, reduces loss or theft, and ensures that
  critical medical tools are always available when needed. It is commonly
  integrated with **custom healthcare software development** to provide
  real-time monitoring and predictive maintenance.
