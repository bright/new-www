question: Why is asset tracking important?
answer: >-
  Asset tracking is important for reducing loss, improving asset utilization,
  and making informed business decisions. Companies lose up to 20% of their
  assets each year due to poor tracking and mismanagement. 


  IoT asset tracking helps you monitor any piece of equipment in real time, so you always know where your devices are. This is especially important in [hospital asset tracking](/projects/hospital-asset-tracking/), where high-value equipment, such as ventilators, infusion pumps, or portable diagnostic tools must be easily located, secured, and maintained.
