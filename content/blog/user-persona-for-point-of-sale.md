## **Meet Jennifer: The Everyday POS Power User**

Jennifer is an energetic waitress with a few years of experience working in a busy dining restaurant. While she doesn’t consider herself particularly tech-savvy, she’s incredibly familiar with her restaurant’s Point-of-Sale system — probably more than anyone else on her team.

She represents a core user group for anyone working on [**POS software development**](https://brightinventions.pl/our-areas/pos-software-development/) efforts: someone who needs a system that’s intuitive, fast, and dependable in high-pressure environments.

## **Why a Persona Like Jennifer Matters for POS Builders**

Whether you’re modernizing your current system or starting fresh with [POS design](https://brightinventions.pl/blog/payment-point-of-sale-design-ui-ux/) & development, Jennifer’s story offers actionable insights. By understanding her workflows, frustrations, and goals, your team can create better tools — and ultimately deliver a smoother, more efficient experience for frontline staff.
