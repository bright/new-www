**The Decentralized Delivery Partners Program, funded by the Polkadot treasury, is an initiative designed to strengthen collaboration between builders and service providers within the Polkadot ecosystem.**

<center>

<TwitterEmbed url='https://x.com/0xBlockDeep/status/1850947694963069095' />

</center>

After a rigorous screening process, we are proud to announce that **Bright Inventions has been included in the [Substrate Delivery Partners directory](https://github.com/blockdeep/delivery-partners)**. This recognition places us among a select group of expert engineering partners capable of building with the Polkadot SDK or setting up the infrastructure to run a chain.

We’re honored that our expertise in Substrate [blockchain development](/our-areas/blockchain/) has been acknowledged, and we look forward to contributing to the growth of the Polkadot ecosystem.

The program's initiator is BlockDeep Labs. Learn more about the Delivery Partners Program on [Block Deep Labs' blog](https://blog.blockdeep.io/launching-the-delivery-partners-directory-394a0dfad607).
