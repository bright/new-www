## 2nd International Conference on Mental Health and Human Resilience

**Date**: July 21-22, 2025

**City**: London, UK

**Ticket pricing**: from 599 USD to 1449 USD.

This global gathering is dedicated to advancing the understanding, support, and advocacy for mental health, with a theme of "Mindfulness in Action: Cultivating Holistic Wellness."

**Details**: [mentalhealth.novelticsconferences.com](https://mentalhealth.novelticsconferences.com/)

## Global Mental Health Research 2025 (MentalHeal2025)

**Date**: July 14-16, 2025

**City**: Phnom Penh, Cambodia

**Ticket pricing**: from 275 USD to 450 USD

MentalHeal2025 is a premier event exploring the latest innovations, research, and strategies in mental health, bringing together professionals, researchers, policymakers, and academics from around the world.

**Details**: [mental.health3000.org](https://mental.health3000.org/)

## Hong Kong International Mental Health Conference – “Navigating Life's Defining Moments”

**Date**: November 6-8, 2025

**City**: Hong Kong

**Ticket pricing**: from 50 USD to 3,800 USD

Mind HK's third conference will focus on the evidence, psychology, and culture of mental health across the lifespan, with a dedicated Mental Wellbeing Festival for the public.

**Details**: [hkmentalhealthconference.com](https://hkmentalhealthconference.com/)

## 7th Global Mental Health Summit

**Date**: November 10-12, 2025

**City**: Cape Town, South Africa

**Ticket pricing**: from 300 USD to 500 USD

This premier event, organized by the Movement for Global Mental Health and the Global Mental Health Peer Network, will bring together experts to discuss the current state and future of mental health, with the theme "Mental Health and Wellbeing - What has Changed? What needs to Change.

**Details**: [www.7thgmhsummit.org](https://www.7thgmhsummit.org/)

## 12th Global Summit on Mental Health and Resilience

**Date**: November 6-7, 2025

**City**: Tokyo, Japan

**Ticket pricing**: from 199 USD to 799 USD

This summit aims to connect mental health leaders, advocates, experts, and academics to discuss new advancements, challenges, and novel findings in mental health and resilience.

**Details**: [idias.org/etn/mhr-2025/](https://idias.org/etn/mhr-2025/)
