## Expect talks from experience iOS developers

Connect with fellow iOS developers, share ideas, enjoy food, and listen to inspiring talks:

**From Melody to Symphony: Coordinators in the World of SwiftUI. Moving beyond declarative navigation toward structure at scale,** Kamil Buczel (Senior iOS developer at Tilt)

SwiftUI’s NavigationStack makes navigation clean and declarative - perfect for small, fresh apps. But real-world projects rarely live in a purely SwiftUI world: UIKit still plays a key role for complex layouts, transitions, and data-heavy screens. This talk shows how to move from declarative navigation to structured, testable flows using the MVVM+C pattern and Coordinators, bridging SwiftUI with UIKit when needed. You’ll learn how coordinators bring clarity, memory safety, and explicit contracts to navigation, helping your app scale with confidence.

**Vibe it and Ship it – Agentic Coding in Practice**, Łukasz Domaradzki (Senior iOS Developer at XTB)

From idea to App Store publication – all with the help of AI agents. During the presentation, Łukasz will talk about how he used Claude Code to write mobile code, detailing what worked great and what the biggest challenges were. Using the Frettier app as an example, he will share lessons learned that can be useful for any developer experimenting with AI.
