## about bright people 🧡

At Bright Inventions (soon becoming Framna), we build digital products that make a real impact. We believe in win by product - creating value through great design, solid engineering, and meaningful collaboration.

We work closely with our clients as true partners sharing ideas, challenging assumptions, and solving problems together. We value people who take ownership, show initiative, and genuinely care about what they build. Clear and kind communication, paired with constructive feedback, helps us all grow together.



## about the project 🧑🏻‍💻🧑🏻‍💻

We’re building our Talent Pool for future QA openings that are expected to become available soon. We value openness and flexibility when it comes to industries; however, the potential project is a rapidly developing mobile application that allows employees use company meal benefits for online orders or in-store payments at restaurants and grocery stores. 

To learn more about this project, feel free to check out our [case study](https://brightinventions.pl/projects/leading-food-delivery-app/).



## your job will include ⚙️

* manually testing Android and iOS applications using Azure DevOps Test Plans
* executing functional, regression and exploratory tests on Android and iOS applications
* analysing network traffic with tools like Charles Proxy or Proxyman
* defining acceptance criteria and creating structured, comprehensive test plans
* estimating, prioritising and coordinating testing activities within the team
* working closely with developers, PMs and UX team to ensure the best product quality
* actively gathering missing requirements and clarifying expectations when needed
* taking part in discussions around UX and suggesting improvements based on your observations
* continuously improving testing practices and documentation



## preferred skills & experience 💪

* min. 3 years of experience in manual testing of mobile applications
* good understanding of network debugging tools (Charles Proxy, Proxyman or similar)
* familiarity with Azure DevOps Test Plans or other test management tools
* good analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to assess the difference of incident severity levels
* curiosity and willingness to ask questions rather than make assumptions
* adaptability to changing requirements and evolving priorities
* user-centric product mindset
* clear and effective communication in both Polish 🇵🇱 and English 🇬🇧



## we would also appreciate 🧡

* hands-on experience with Appium for mobile test automation
